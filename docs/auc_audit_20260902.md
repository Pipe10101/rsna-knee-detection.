# AUC audit, 2026-09-02 — what is measured, where the headroom is, and the ranked path to 0.90

> **Status 2026-09-07**: sessions 1-2 measured everything ranked here. The lever that landed was one the audit under-ranked — **16 epochs, +0.0160, adopted**; zoomc is a small repeatable +0.003; per-label distillation, layout ensemble, multi-bag inference, seed ensembling and g10t3 are closed. Current state and launch order live at the top of `docs/slotknee_runbook.md`.

Every number below is either measured in this repo or quoted from the competition forum.
Nothing is an estimate unless it says so.

## 1. Where we stand

| | pooled OOF (4407) | vs 58 expert labels, macro-10 | public LB |
|---|---|---|---|
| champion (Aug 23, coverage only) | 0.8531 | 0.851 | **0.866** |
| **new 5-fold** (coverage + reg4 + distil 0.5 + bag6) | **0.8643** | **0.871** | not yet entered |

LB has tracked pooled OOF ~1:1 all campaign, with a +0.013 ensemble/TTA gap — so the new
model should land ~0.875-0.880. The distilled OOF is inflated by cross-fold teacher
information, which is why the expert-label column is the honest one: **+0.020 macro-10 on
the same 58 studies**, and every one of the 12 findings moved in the right direction.

The field (forum, self-reported single/5-fold models): 0.947 (4th, small model, **also top of the
Efficiency LB**, "spent a lot of time working around the low quality labels"), 0.942 (5-fold
**@ 224 px**), 0.929 (LLM labels only, 224 px), 0.915 (DINOv2, random bag of 32 slices).
Same backbone family and the same resolution as ours reach 0.94. **Resolution is closed;
the differentiators are labels handling and what the model is shown.**

To reach 0.90 LB we need pooled OOF ≈ 0.885-0.890: **+0.020 to +0.025**. No single lever
measured in this campaign is that large; the two biggest ever were coverage (+0.0325) and
reg4 (+0.0220). This is a stack of gated wins, not one idea.

| Effusion | 0.981 | 0.877 | +0.104 | beats its labels |

| Lateral OA / Synovitis | 0.752 / 0.798 | 0.833 / 0.790 | — | gold known-corrupt: never tune |

Three conclusions the table forces:

1. **The remaining gap is an image-reading problem in five findings, and four of them are
   coronal joint-line structures.** The same five were model-limited on the old champion; the
   new recipe narrowed every gap (MCL +0.066, LatMen +0.076) without changing the shape.
   Averaging more evidence better (coverage, distillation, bagging — all variance reduction,
   mutually correlated at r up to 0.83) is now largely spent. What is left needs **new
   information**: a dedicated coronal joint-line view.
2. **Uniform distillation is now provably wrong-signed on half the labels.** The model is a
   better teacher than its labels on Fracture/Effusion/Contusion/MedOA and a far worse one on
   ACL/MCL/MedMen/LatMen. `--distill-weight 0.5` everywhere pulls the second group toward a
   weaker target. Per-label weights cost nothing at inference and the flag already exists.
3. **Label refinement is closed.** `llm_labels_v6_refined` changed five columns on thousands
   of rows yet scores *identically* to `v4_blend` against expert gold (0.893 / 0.909); the
   `r4refined` arm's apparent +0.011 came from scoring against its own altered targets. The
   only statistically significant label win ever measured is the Fracture re-extraction
   (+0.010), and the model now beats its Fracture labels by 0.156 anyway.

## 4b. Session 2 (2026-09-06) — the lever the audit under-ranked

**16 epochs: +0.0160 pooled (gate 0.0011), ADOPTED.** Longer training was listed as +0.005–0.012 at 45 %; it landed at the top of that range because bagging turns each epoch into new views. Val AUC is still rising at 16, so `recipe_e24` leads session 3. Noise floor on this layout re-measured at **0.0005**. Multi-bag inference and seed ensembling are closed (−0.002 and +0.0007). `cor_spec` regressed overall but read MCL **+0.0215**, keeping the per-label slot prior alive.

## 5. Efficiency (the constraint, not the lever)

Measured on a T4: decode 0.21 s/study (dicomsdl), model 0.078 s per member-forward → the
5-member accuracy entry with 3-shift TTA is 1.18 s/study, ~30 min for 1300 studies; without
TTA ~13 min. Exchange rate 0.01 AUC ≈ 12 min. Every lever ranked 2, 4, 5, 6, 8 above is
**free at inference**; only zoomc (+33 %) and a layout ensemble (2×) cost runtime, and both
survive the exchange rate. The Efficiency LB is led at **0.947** — it is won on accuracy.
For that entry: distil the 5-fold into ONE student (1.18 → 0.08 s/study), no TTA, DINOv3-S if
its ~23 % encoder saving prices neutral.

## 6. What this audit found wrong and fixed on the way

* silent round-robin fold fallback (would inflate CV ~0.053 with no log line) — fixed, tested
* unseeded multi-bag inference (irreproducible submissions) — fixed
* ablate kernel measuring every arm on the pre-campaign recipe — fixed, verified by pull-back
* `check_gpu()` before input discovery (CPU pushes proved nothing) — fixed; pre-flights now
  verify every cache name
* `refined` labels adopted nowhere and now shown to be NULL vs gold — closed
